Cash drawer (Windows till only)

For a cash drawer plugged into the receipt printer. It opens for cash sales, cash refunds and the 💷 Open drawer button - no paper is printed. Start the till with "Start Till.bat" so the drawer helper is running.

Backups

All your products and sales live on this PC only. Back up to a USB stick or external drive regularly. If the PC fails without a backup, the data is gone.

About

Shop Till works offline. When the PC is online, unknown barcodes are looked up on Open Food Facts (a free product database) to fill in the name. Prices always come from you or your price file.
